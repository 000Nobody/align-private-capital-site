Align Private Capital LLC is a women-owned, SEC registered investment advisor founded in 2014. We make direct investments on behalf of, or in conjunction with, single family offices through an absolute return fund.

Our origins

Founded on a simple conviction.

Align was founded in 2014 to make direct investments for and alongside single family offices. From the outset, the firm focused on a simple conviction: families who have made money in a specific vertical are the best experts in those fields, and are well positioned to identify opportunities before institutional investors.

Over more than 30 years, Align's principals have assembled a proprietary network of 100+ single family offices and sector experts, providing sourcing and partnership opportunities, and a first look at transactions that never reach the broader market.

Our funds

One strategy, evolving vehicles.

Align invests through an absolute return strategy that deploys capital across asset classes, security types and geographies. That strategy has been expressed through a succession of fund vehicles.

2019

Current I

Align's first fund: an absolute return, flexible-capital fund launched in partnership with a single limited partner, investing across asset classes with a focus on risk-adjusted returns, income and downside protection.

2021

Evergreen Conversion

In August 2021, Current I was converted to a permanent-capital, evergreen vehicle, supporting a long-term investment horizon and the firm's growing pipeline.

2025

APC Fund II

Align Private Capital Fund II LP invests side by side with Current I and employs the same strategy, taking advantage of the firm's growing investment pipeline.

Shared conviction

Invested side by side.

When the families who source an opportunity also commit alongside our partners, incentives are shared and conviction is real. That discipline shapes everything we do.

It extends to how we work: partner consensus on every decision, transparent reporting, and a long-term orientation that puts the durability of relationships ahead of any single transaction.
