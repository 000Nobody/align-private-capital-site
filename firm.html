An investment firm
built around its network.

Align Private Capital makes three to four direct investments per year, each shaped by rigorous due diligence and sourced from a proprietary network of more than 100 single family offices. The firm often invests alongside families who know their sectors best, across asset classes, security types and geographies.

Our origins

Founded on a simple conviction.

Align was founded in 2014 to make direct investments for and alongside single family offices. From the outset, the firm focused on a simple conviction: families who have been successful in a specific vertical are the best experts in those fields, and are well positioned to identify opportunities before institutional investors.

Over more than 30 years, Align's principals have assembled a proprietary network of 100+ single family offices and sector experts, providing sourcing and partnership opportunities, and a first look at transactions that are proprietary.

Our strategy

One strategy, multiple investment vehicles.

Align invests through an absolute return strategy that deploys capital across asset classes, security types and geographies, seeking risk-adjusted returns, income and downside protection. Over the firm's history, that strategy has been expressed through a series of funds and special purpose vehicles supporting a long-term investment horizon and a growing pipeline of opportunities.

Shared conviction

Invested side by side.

When the families who source an opportunity also make significant capital commitments alongside our partners, incentives are aligned.

Every decision is made by partner consensus, with transparent reporting, and a long-term orientation that puts the durability of relationships ahead of any single transaction.
