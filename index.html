Who we are

A women-owned, SEC registered investment advisor.

Founded in 2014, Align makes direct investments on behalf of, or in conjunction with, single family offices ("SFOs") through an absolute return fund, drawing on a sourcing network refined over three decades.

Align believes that families who have made money in a specific vertical are the best experts in those fields, and hold a unique ability to source opportunities in their sectors before they reach the broader market.
